# The World in One Day — Saturday 21 November 2026

One 1024×1024 front page summing up Saturday 21 November 2026: 35 stories
across 7 regions (Europe 13 · Asia 5 · Middle East 6 · Africa 1 ·
Americas 5 · Oceania 2 · Global 3), every one dated that day and sourced.
Three heroes (COP31 overtime, Cyclone Akvan, Peru quake 6.9), ten panels,
a dark world map with region-coloured numbered pins, 22 more stories in
ALSO, and a DATE CHECK box listing Friday's news that was left out.

v5 grows the type for legibility at 300px (hero heads 20px, hero labels
30px, panel heads 16px, ALSO 12px) and reduces every illustration to one
big flat icon.

`main.ts` copies the shipped `entry.png` byte-for-byte to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
